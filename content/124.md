## NightHacking I/O Things Tour- Oct 18 to Nov 19 2013

 

From October 18th to November 19th I will be motorcycling across Europe and doing live video streaming of the journey. Along the way I will visit user groups, conferences, and, interview interesting folks, and hack on open source projects. The last stop will be the [Devoxx conference](http://devoxx.be/) in Belgium, which is the largest Java conference in Europe\!

Join me for the journey by watching the live stream in 3 easy steps:

1. Follow [@nighthackingtv](http://twitter.com/nighthackingtv) on Twitter to find out when we go live
1. Come to the NightHacking site to watch the free live feed

Here are some of the countries I plan to visit:

- Munich
- Italy
- France
- Spain
- United Kingdom
- The Netherlands
- Belgium

You can also become part of the event for signing up join me in your home country as I pass through. To sign-up, please fill out the "[Let's Meetup Form](https://steveonjava.com/travel-map/lets-meetup/)" and let me know which city you would like to meet in.
